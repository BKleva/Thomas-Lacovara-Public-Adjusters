Now hiring: part-time public adjuster.

South Jersey's public adjuster since 1997 is expanding its team. We're looking for an experienced insurance claims professional to take on public adjusting claims, from first contact through final settlement.

Part-Time Public Adjuster (Experienced Claims Professional)

  • Linwood, NJ · South Jersey
  • Part-time
  • $18,000 – $50,000 per year

Who we're looking for

  • 8+ years of experience in insurance claims, or a recently retired insurance adjuster or claims professional
  • Willing to obtain a New Jersey public adjuster license
  • Able to handle the full public adjusting process: reviewing policies, documenting and scoping damage, preparing estimates and claim packages, negotiating with insurance carriers, and closing out settlements
  • Strong communicator, organized, and comfortable working independently
  • Experience with fire, water, wind, or storm losses is a plus

Why join us

  • Flexible part-time schedule
  • Established firm with a strong reputation and a steady stream of claims
  • Support from a team with decades of public adjusting experience

How to apply

Email your resume and a few days/times you're available for a quick call to tlacfire@gmail.com with the subject line “Public Adjuster Application.”
